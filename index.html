Deep Q-Network vs LRU

Solo research project

An L2 cache simulator with a small neural network that decides which block to evict. After fixing the network's blind spot to block order and shaping its reward around real DRAM costs, it reached 98.9 cycles average memory access time against LRU's 101.5.
